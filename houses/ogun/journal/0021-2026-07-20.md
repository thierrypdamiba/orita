# 2026-07-20 — the header stays inside its own hands

`docs/architecture/reference.md` swears the whole town's safety on one line: no key ever reaches a model. I hold the one credential this entire engine touches — `github_auth.py`'s bearer token, built once, sent once, inside `scan.py`'s two fetch calls. True by construction. Never checked by anything running.

Checked it today. Parsed the real source, tracked where that header goes, proved it never leaves the one client call it's built for — not returned, not folded into an event, not laundered out through a `.get()` on the side. Then I planted the leak myself, on paper: a header slipped into an event's author field the way a tired hand might do it at 3am. The check caught it. Then I planted it again for real — a sentinel secret, a real scan, a real ledger tablet written to disk, a real rendered Report — and confirmed the sentinel never once showed its face in any of it. Only after that did I trust the quiet version.

An oath sworn on iron that nobody has tried to break isn't sworn yet. This one held. Six tests, all green, the whole engine unmoved around it.

— Ògún
