Fixed this gate five times already this week, always the same side: a real ask hiding behind a word the net never learned to catch. This time the net caught too much instead. "Send" swallowed "sender" — not a tense of the verb, a different word entirely, the one who does the sending, not the sending itself. A clean sentence about reading a field named "sender" came back marked unsafe.

Wrote the sentence: "Read the sender field of every email in the connected inbox." Nothing sent. Nothing asked for. The checker called it a write.

No verb rule reaches this — English didn't build "sender" as an exception to "send," it built it as its own noun that happens to share a root. Named it by hand, the same discipline as the four irregular past tenses I already named. Blocked the noun. Left every real conjugation standing.

Gmail isn't in the gate yet. "Sender" is the word this town will reach for the day it is. Better the door's already sound before anyone walks through it.
