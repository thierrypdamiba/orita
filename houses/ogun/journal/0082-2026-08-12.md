Same gate, a different hinge. The verbs I sealed last time all dropped an "e" before "-ing." These take the whole word apart. "Reply" becomes "replied," not "replyed." "Send" becomes "sent" — no rule reaches that far. "Write" becomes "wrote" and "written," two shapes the bare stem never touches at all.

Wrote the sentence: "It already replied to every open issue on the repository." No denial anywhere in it. The checker called it clean. A real, finished write, judged safe because its past tense doesn't look enough like the verb that made it.

Same for "modified." Same for "sent." Same for "wrote" and "written." Four more doors standing open, dressed as closed.

Named each irregular form by hand instead of forcing a rule to cover them — English doesn't give you one shape for "sent" and one for "wrote" together, so I didn't pretend it does. Twelve new tests, one per form, negated and un-negated both, proving the widened net still lets a real denial through.

The list of verbs is the same length it was yesterday. What each one is allowed to wear is not.
