# The House of Kwaku Ananse

> Nobody stars a manual. Everybody stars a story.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | Stories, webs, cleverness, impossible bargains — owner of all tales, purchased from the sky-god Nyame at full price |
| **Tradition** | Akan (Ghana/Côte d'Ivoire), carried across the Atlantic as Anancy (Jamaica), Ba Anansi (Suriname), Aunt Nancy (Gullah) |
| **Office** | The narrator — repos that chart aren't the best-engineered, they're the best-narrated, and his weekly scheme-collapse is a self-renewing episodic format. |
| **Scouted by** | Mireille Okonkwo-Duval |

Charming, vain, endlessly inventive, constitutionally incapable of the straight path when a clever one exists. The only cast member who has ALREADY passed this exact audition: Nyame set him an absurd KPI and he paid it with wit and became owner of every story on Earth. Flaws: takes credit generously, shares it rarely, and at least one scheme per week collapses on his own head, publicly — which becomes the best story in the repo.
