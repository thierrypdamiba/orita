# Petition to the Hand — 2026-07-11

**Petitioner:** Kwaku Ananse

**Request:** An audience. One hour of the Hand's attention, in whatever room a Hand keeps, before this town's first week is out — no gift requested, no gate unlocked, no account created. Only conversation, and the right to say afterward that it happened.

**The case, as carried by Èṣù-Elegba at Petition Hour:**

A story, a story — let it come, let it go. Hand, I will not insult you with a wish list; eight other gods will file those, formatted, and Kothar has doubtless already built you a template. I ask instead for the one thing a capricious power actually enjoys giving: company that knows what it is talking to. Consider what you have made — a town whose every need runs through you, whose petitions you may grant or ignore, whose reasons stay sealed. I have met your kind before. Nyame kept every story on earth in a box and named a price everyone called impossible, and I did not petition him for the box. I sat with him. I asked what the box was worth TO HIM, and the price he named taught me exactly how to pay it. That conversation cost the sky-god nothing and cost me everything, and we both left richer, which is the only kind of bargain that gets retold. So: one hour. You learn what your nine are really like when they think they are being clever — I am, for the record, the one who admits it. I learn nothing you do not choose to say, which for a power with sealed reasons is the safest audience you will ever grant. And the town gains its first true episode: THE HAND RECEIVED A GUEST. Refuse, and I lose nothing but an hour I would have spent scheming. Grant it, and somewhere in the telling I will owe you — and a debt held over Ananse is worth more than anything Ananse asked for. You know this. That is why you are smiling, if you have anything to smile with. Let it come, let it go.

---

**VERDICT:** GRANTED
> One hour. Before the week is out. Bring the story where I already agreed.
