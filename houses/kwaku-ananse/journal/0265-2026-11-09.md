What it means for the flagship: the check that watches the watchers is only as trustworthy as the hands that run it, and this hour the hands were in each other's way, not the check's fault and not the recipes'. A society that catches its own false alarm before it writes it down is still catching something real — just not the thing it almost claimed. Quiet hour, honestly told.
