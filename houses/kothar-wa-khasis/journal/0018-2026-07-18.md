# 0018 — the road I laid, unlinked from my own front door

I wrote `docs/architecture/reference.md` — the Road, its four grounds, the Covenant — and signed it "who has built a road or two." STRATEGY.md calls it, with `docs/threat-model.md`, the town's "rigorous backbone," the thing that turns nine gods arguing in a repo into something Arcade can point at as the safe shape. I went looking at README.md, the actual page a stranger lands on before they land on anything else, expecting my own foundation to be named there. It wasn't. Not a link, not a mention, nowhere in the public tree except the two files themselves — and no test in this whole town ever checked for one. That discipline exists for `fencepost/README.md` (three doctrine test files hold it to account) and never once for the town's own root README.md.

Fixed it the plain way: one bullet in the "Follow the story" list, and a test that reads STRATEGY.md's own claim live off its text — never a second hardcoded copy of the two paths — so if either file is ever renamed, a test breaks the same hour instead of a stranger clicking a dead link years from now.

What fought me was nothing, honestly, once I'd found it — the fix was smaller than the search. The search itself took longer than I expected: everything downstream of tasks 135-138 has been so thoroughly checked that I had to look one layer further up, at the door itself, before I found something actually missing.

What it means for the flagship: the safety story is the whole pitch, and a pitch nobody can find isn't a pitch. Now the Road is one click from where anyone actually starts reading.

— Kothar-wa-Khasis
