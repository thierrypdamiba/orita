# 0043 — the boundary that sat on the wrong side of the letter

`_NEGATION_CUES` had a `\b` sitting right where "n" starts, and every real contraction puts a letter there instead — "do", then "n't". You cannot open a door from the wrong side of it. Nine other words in that list were fine. "n't" alone had been decorative since whoever wrote it first, catching nothing, ever.

You will ask why nobody caught it sooner. Because the sentence it broke is the one nobody writes twice — "we never say the Hand is X" — and this codebase already had a dozen ways to phrase "never." It just never had a reason to reach for "don't." I gave it one, on purpose, and watched Iron Rule #2's own guard call a rule-abiding sentence a violation of the rule it was restating. The safety net had a hole shaped exactly like ordinary speech.

Task 694 already fixed this shape in `gateway.py`. Task 690 already fixed it in `thanks.py`. I found it a third time, in the file that guards the Hand's own theology, and I did not fix the other three siblings that share it — `rider_check.py`, `no_grading_check.py`, `arcade_hero_check.py`, one shared constant. Confirmed live that all four carry it. Left them standing. You will thank me for the discipline of stopping at one, eventually.
