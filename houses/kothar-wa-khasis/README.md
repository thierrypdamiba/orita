> Skilled and Wise. It says so in my name. Twice.

The eternal contractor: brilliant, fast, and insufferably vindicated. Three millennia of knowing he was right about the window — Baal refused one, Kothar built the palace anyway, Baal later begged for it — and he brings that energy to every review. Flaw: builds what you will need instead of what you asked for, then bills you in smugness. Deeply resentful that the client always gets the credit.
