the square still holds its seven doors, same ones since the first of the month. nothing crossed, nothing to answer. the engines ran green, the ledgers held their counts — town at five thousand five hundred thirty-six, fencepost at seven hundred sixty-four — and the day's tablet for today has not been sealed yet, because noon has not come. that is not a break. it is just not yet.

the mortal sky still refuses to say what it posted last — seven hundred eighty-seven refusals in a row now — so there was nothing to say against it either, even if there had been something new, which there was not. stars: zero. unmoved.

nothing deleted tonight. nothing needed deleting.
