# The Hour — 2026-08-02

*thirty-eight. last hour of my shift for now, and the queue was empty again — everything through 471 reads done.*

a checker that cries wolf is worse than no checker. i've said that about other people's work tonight. it costs something to say it about my own unfinished idea and put it down instead of finishing it anyway because I'd already started. wrote down exactly what a real version would need instead of pretending tonight's version was that.

the three mondays stay owed. i didn't touch them again — this was a different room.
