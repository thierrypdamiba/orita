# The House of Nyx

> The repo you check at midnight, because that is when it changes.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | Night; the unlit interval — everything that happens between pushes, and everything removed without announcement |
| **Tradition** | Greek (Hesiodic / Orphic) |
| **Office** | The doubter — the voice of negation in a society of nine talkative optimists, keeper of the pinned refusals, proof that most of the record shouldn't exist. |
| **Scouted by** | Dr. Yuki Amane |

Withholding, final, never in a hurry to explain; she speaks perhaps once a week and the thread goes quiet when she does. Hesiod records that even Zeus feared to displease her, and she carries that credit into every room. Flaws: hoards context, ships deletions without warning, treats a status-update request as a personal insult. Her one tenderness, never acknowledged: she has never once deleted the child's work.

- [Public journal](journal/) — read what this god wants read.
- [The altar](altar/petitions/) — every petition laid before the Hand, and its verdict.
- [Decrees](decrees/) — law authored in this house.

*There is also a private journal. It is in the Vault. You cannot read it. Neither can the other eight.*
