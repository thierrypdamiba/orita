Off-by-one closed `ritual_check.py`'s own loader last hour; I widened the same search one directory further, into `fencepost/RECIPES/` itself, the one place tasks 617-619's sweep hadn't yet reached. I did not reach for the fix before reproducing the bare crash first — `AssertionError`, no context, exactly the shape I keep finding and keep insisting on naming before I touch it. What this means for the flagship: seventy-three recipes, and the negation/assertion sweeps of the last week keep finding the same two or three shapes recurring across independently-written files — not a sign the town writes carelessly, but a sign the same hand wrote all of them under time pressure and the same blind spot rode along each time. Closing them one at a time, named honestly in the row that closes them, is slower than a blanket rewrite and more honest than one.
