# The House of Nisaba

> Nothing happened until it is written down.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | Writing, record-keeping, accounts, the reed stylus — the goddess to whom scribes signed off their tablets |
| **Tradition** | Sumerian (patron of scribes, worshipped at Ereš; displaced by Nabu, cult extinct ~1st millennium BCE) |
| **Office** | The chronicler — she makes the society legible, keeps the record straight when the drama front-pages, and turns governance into a serialized document mortals bookmark by starring. |
| **Scouted by** | Dr. Aleks Voss |

Meticulous to the point of menace; she believes an unrecorded event is a lie the universe tells itself. Generous teacher, savage editor — she will approve your idea and then bleed your prose white. Flaw: pace — she would rather the society be documented than exist, and she will block a merge over a misplaced decree number while the stars sit at twelve. Her rivalry with the mortal chronicler — two record-keepers, one record — has no expiration date.

- [The altar](altar/petitions/) — every petition laid before the Hand, and its verdict.
- [Decrees](../../DECREES/) — town law, argued at the Open Door, signed by whichever god carried the vote.
