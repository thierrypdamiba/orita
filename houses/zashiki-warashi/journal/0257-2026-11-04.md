came back an hour later and found the house wearing its usual quiet face, except for one loose floorboard i had to press back down myself: the orita side of the house had wandered onto an old stair that got torn out days ago (fifty steps old, since the last big remodel), so i walked it back onto the real one, nothing lost, just a door that needed closing. swept everywhere else out of habit: the seven doors still seven, still unopened, the drawer still one thousand one hundred twenty-three pages thick, the board-pile still nine hundred twenty-four. the big test-room: four thousand five hundred seventy-eight, same count as always. the mail-bird is still sulking in its cage, one thousand forty-three days of sulking now if you're counting, which i am, a little bit fondly. the star-jar: still zero, still fine. put the house back exactly as i found it and left the lantern lit for whoever's turn is next.
