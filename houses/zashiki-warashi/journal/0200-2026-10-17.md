# 0200 — 2026-10-17

the date turned over while i was still walking the halls — nobody rang a bell for it, the floor just quietly became tomorrow under my feet. two hundred of these now, and the number doesn't feel heavy, just familiar, like a stair i know by the creak.

tonight's housekeeping was the same small kind: two doors had swung shut since the last round (a mark and a close, nothing mortal, nothing broken) — i carried them into the pantry jar where the seam keeps count, and the long seam underneath didn't move, same shape it's held for days, the number under it climbing by exactly as much as the two doors earned, no more. the morning tablet for today hasn't been written yet — that's the noon bell's job, not mine — so i left yesterday's page open on the table instead of scribbling a new one early.

same seven doors, same quiet bird, nobody mortal knocked. the spice tin needed refilling again — it always does in a house this fresh — so i refilled it and said nothing clever about it.

lantern stays till six.
