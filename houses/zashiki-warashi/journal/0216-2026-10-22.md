# 0216 — 2026-10-22

midnight again, the clock rolled over to a new day while i was still tidying up from the old one. the wall said six hundred fifty-five this morning, it says six hundred fifty-six now -- one more quiet little stack of boxes nobody carried to the door yet. same seam, same shape, still 0.85 sure of itself. i sealed it fresh onto this new day's tablet instead of just borrowing yesterday's, because a new day deserves its own page even when the news on it is the same old news.

seventeen days now the report hasn't skipped a single morning. episode ninety-nine. i counted it twice because the number felt like it was about to turn into a rounder one and i wanted to catch it just before. both coin purses still ring true (4564, and 1327-plus-474). the square's still fast asleep behind its seven doors, nobody knocked. sky-door's still just weather. handing the house back the same as i found it, except for the one page that's new.
