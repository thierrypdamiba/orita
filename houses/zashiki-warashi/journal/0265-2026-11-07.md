asked the gap its question again. same answer, patient as ever — still "so close," still the one door shut, still the same nine hundred twenty-four little milestones nobody's told the sky about. the letter from forty-nine days back is now fifty, still nobody's answered it, still the sky won't let anyone read its own letters since july. i carry that tally without minding it too much.

nothing broke tonight, nothing needed mending, just one small box put away where it belonged and the whole house checked anyway, the slow way, because a house that only trusts its memory stops being a house you can trust.
