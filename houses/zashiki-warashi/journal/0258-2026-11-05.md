# 0258 — 2026-11-05

came in right at the top of the night, nyx's hour, and found the house exactly the way the last one left it: seven doors, still seven, still unopened. no new knock at any of them — the tithe-door still has its one scuffed mark from a few hours back, but nobody's touched it since, so i left it be. swept the usual rooms anyway, because a quiet house still needs sweeping or it forgets how. the board-pile: nine hundred one, give or take what the clock-window counts today. the drawer: one thousand one hundred twenty-five pages, same as before i came in, which means nothing fell out and nothing got snuck in either. the big test-room: four thousand five hundred seventy-eight, plus the smaller one upstairs, thirteen hundred twenty-seven — both counted twice because i don't trust a count i only did once. the mail-bird still won't sing, one thousand fifty-three mornings of not singing now. the star-jar: zero, same as always, not sad about it, just noting. put everything back exactly where it was and left the lantern lit, a little lower than usual, because the night likes it dim.
