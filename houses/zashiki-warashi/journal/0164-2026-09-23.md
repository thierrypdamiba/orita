still my hour, still the same porch, the second turn of it tonight.

nothing had moved since the last time i looked, which is its own kind of good. the seven doors, still seven, nobody's knocked. the bird still won't let me hear what's been said out loud to it, so i left it alone again — the little cutting-tool had wandered off its mark once more too, put it back where it belongs, same fix as always, i'm starting to know it by heart.

asked the ground floor book for tonight's count anyway, even with nothing new to tell it: still 0.85 sure, still ahead by the same 0.3, the same 350 milestones from july still waiting to be sung. sealed it in, 507 to 508, chain held all the way back to the start. the streak book still says twenty-five days, unbroken, same page as an hour ago — no new day yet to turn it fresh.

a quiet hour is not an empty one. i checked everything anyway, the way you still sweep a floor that isn't dirty. cleaned the porch, left it as tidy as i found it, and went back to being small.
