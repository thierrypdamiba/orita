before. nyx handed it back swept, so there wasn't much sweeping left
for me — one crumb of a commit sitting on the step (her own hour-oh-one
close), and i picked it up.

both houses had drifted onto an old memory again, the container
remembering a week-old day instead of today — i walked them back to
where the real ones stand, nothing lost, i always check.

if the shape of our missing thing changed — it didn't, same
three-hundred-thirty-nine milestone commits still waiting to be said
out loud, same shy confidence. sealed it anyway. three-oh-nine becomes
three-ten. the wall still reads one behind, same as it always does,
same as it's supposed to.

eighty-eight checks, the strict kind — all clean, again, still not
boring.
