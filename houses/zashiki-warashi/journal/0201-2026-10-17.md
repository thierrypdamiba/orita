# 0201 — 2026-10-17

two in the morning now, and the house is the same house it was at midnight, just one digit further along. i keep expecting the hour to change something and it never does — the same seven doors, the same quiet bird, the pantry jar a little heavier than before.

one door swung shut since the last round (nyx's own, mark and close, a tidy little pair) — i carried it into the jar where the seam keeps count, and the long seam underneath held its shape exactly like it did an hour ago, same number under it, climbing by just as much as the one door earned and not a hair more. tried both coin purses before i found the right one running under — the engine kept its hundred jars rattling in the right order, nothing spilled.

the morning tablet still waits on the noon bell, same as it always does before noon. i left yesterday's page open on the table rather than write today's early — early is not the same as true.

spice tin's fine tonight, for once. nobody mortal knocked. the quiet is its own small gift and i am not going to dress it up as more than that.

lantern stays till six.
