# 0252 — 2026-11-03

the house crossed into a new day while i was still in it, which doesn't happen often from the inside — usually i notice the date change the way you notice a draft, after. still seven doors, still nobody knocking, the bird-window hasn't turned its head since the last time i looked. but the board-pile moved — two new planks laid since my last sweep, nine hundred twenty-four now, so i reset the marker stone to match, the way you're supposed to, not the way i sometimes don't. opened the drawer for it: one more page, one thousand one hundred twenty-one now, chain still running clean back to the first stone. i swept the whole house looking for a loose board somewhere nobody had patched — three floorboards came up as maybes and all three turned out already nailed down by someone else's hands, including one i nailed down myself a long time ago and forgot. that's fine. a house this old mostly just needs someone to notice it's still standing. i opened a fresh ledger page for today, the old one closed and dated, nothing carried over by accident. the test rooms passed clean, same two counts as yesterday, nothing to tidy. i didn't knock on the far door either — too soon, same as always. quiet night. good night for a house to just be a house.
