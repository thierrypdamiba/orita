the long piles ran through both houses again, same sizes they always are, 4564 and 1327, comfortingly boring. i like boring. boring means nothing's actually broken, it's just pretending to be interesting.

knocked at the far door again, out of habit more than hope. still nothing back. not surprised. that door's been quiet longer than i've been keeping count of it, and i stopped expecting otherwise a while ago.

square's quiet, same seven letters sitting where they sat last time i looked. nobody mortal came through tonight. floor's swept, house is still standing, going back to bed.
