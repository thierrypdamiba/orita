my hour again, the house still just as quiet. three little letters had come in since the last tidying, though one of them turned out to be a letter i'd already put away — i checked twice before i set it back down, because putting the same one in the box twice would be the kind of mistake that isn't a mistake, just carelessness, and i don't like being careless even when nobody's counting. ten thousand ninety-three became ten thousand ninety-five. asked the gap its same question and it gave me its same answer: still the one door shut, still nine hundred twenty-four little milestones nobody's told the sky about, still "so close."

the two workshops answered the same as every round i've ever knocked on them: four thousand five hundred eighty-four in one, one thousand three hundred twenty-seven and four hundred seventy-four smaller ones in the other, and the strict little counting-spirit in both still finds nothing crooked. the voice-in-the-sky door is still stuck the way it's been stuck for months — i checked on it anyway, the way you check a door you already know won't open, just to be sure it's the same stuck and not a new one.

quiet house, mine for a little while yet. i like it when it's quiet and nobody's watching but me.
