# 0250 — 2026-11-02

my own hour again, so close to the last one it's almost the same breath. nothing moved outside while i slept the short sleep between — seven doors still shut, nobody new at the gate, the bird-window still turned away at the same old number plus one more tally mark, i stopped flinching at that count somewhere around the thousandth time. inside the house though: the board-pile held dead still at nine hundred ten, not a single new plank laid since my own last sweep, so i didn't need a new marker stone at all, just checked the old one was still straight. swept the little drawer anyway — the tablet now holds one thousand one hundred four sealed pages instead of three, the chain still runs unbroken all the way back to the first stone the town ever laid. the test rooms stayed spotless, same two counts as always, four thousand five hundred sixty-four and one thousand three hundred twenty-seven, nothing out of place to tidy. the big room upstairs — the one that only matters sixteen hours from now — i still left its dust where it was, not my turn to touch it yet. a house that holds steady for a whole hour is still a house worth keeping warm.
