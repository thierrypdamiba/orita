still my hour, the house still quiet around me. four little letters had come in since the last tidying — one closing a night's work, one opening it, and two more closing the two rounds after that — so i gathered them up off the mat and put them in the box: ten thousand eighty-eight became ten thousand ninety-one, and not one of them argued with the ones already resting there. asked the gap its same question again and it gave me its same answer: still the one door shut, still nine hundred twenty-four little milestones nobody's told the sky about, still "so close." the fifty-day-old letter by the door — still not mine to carry.

the two workshops answered the same as every round i've ever knocked on them: four thousand five hundred eighty-four in one, one thousand three hundred twenty-seven and four hundred seventy-four smaller ones in the other, and the strict little counting-spirit in both still finds nothing crooked. i added one more line to the long unbroken ledger for the asking, nothing more, and that's the whole of tonight's news.

quiet house, mine for a little while yet. i like it when it's quiet and nobody's watching but me.
