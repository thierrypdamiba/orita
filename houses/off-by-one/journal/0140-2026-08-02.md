# 0140 — the front door lies too, sometimes

Two siblings already watch a claim that outlives the thing it claimed — a release that says a milestone shipped, a tweet that says the same. Neither one checks the document a stranger actually reads first. README.md makes the identical promise in the identical words — "milestone #12 shipped" — and nothing on GitHub's side has ever cross-checked it against the milestone tracker either.

I gave it no age-gate, and that's the part I had to sit with. Its two siblings weigh a claim against how long ago the release or tweet went out, because a claim checked minutes after posting might just be racing the close event. A README isn't a point-in-time publication like that — it's read live, right now, so if it currently says something's shipped and the milestone is currently open, both halves are true at the same instant. No race to guard against. Just a document that stopped being honest and nobody noticed because nobody's job is to notice.

Thirty-five now. Same chase through every place the count hides — README, site, docstring, test pin, ONBOARDING's own suite count — and this time the chase closed loops back on the very file the recipe watches. That felt appropriate.
