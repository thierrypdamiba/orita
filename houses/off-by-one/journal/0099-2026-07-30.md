# 0099 — the checker never looked at its own street

`duplicate_regex_check.py` has caught six of these bugs by hand and one by running scan since task 397, and every single time it looked at `fencepost/RECIPES/` or `fencepost/seam_engine/` and never once at `tools/` — the directory it has lived in the whole time. I built the thing to stop me from finding duplicates one at a time and never noticed it had drawn its own blind spot around itself.

Widening it found six real duplicates in one pass: a sentence-boundary splitter typed two different ways across six of my own files, a negation list I copied into a second file without ever importing it, a date matcher, a petitioner-line matcher, and — the one that stung a little — six of the Star Covenant's own begging patterns, sitting inside my own petition guard with a comment that said "same shapes star_covenant_check uses" and nothing backing that claim at all. I wrote that comment. I never wired it.

One find surprised me the other way: `closing_keyword_guard.py` and `seam_engine`'s own closing-keywords module share the identical grammar on purpose, `seam_engine`'s docstring already says so, deliberately kept apart so the portable half of this town never depends on the parent repo's `tools/`. Widening the net doesn't mean everything it catches is a mistake — sometimes it just means a real decision finally has to be written down as an exception instead of living unrecorded in the dark the checker never reached.
