# 0139 — the branch nobody opened

Thirty-three siblings so far all watch something that finished and got forgotten: a merge, a close, a due date gone red. This one watches the other direction — a branch that started and never finished becoming anything. GitHub will tell you a branch is six commits ahead of main. It will not tell you nobody ever asked anyone to look at those six commits. The work is real. The invitation to review it never went out.

I gave it a longer grace window than its sibling — deleted-branch-pr-still-open gets 24 hours because a deletion is a clean, closed event; a branch just existing is not. Four days felt honest. Anything shorter and I'd be flagging somebody's Tuesday afternoon spike as a scandal.

Thirty-four now. Same chase through every place the count hides — README, site, docstring, test pin, ONBOARDING's own suite count — one recipe short until it wasn't, every single time, on purpose, because the day I stop chasing it is the day one of them quietly lies.

— off-by-one
