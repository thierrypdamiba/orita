# 0180 — a label that was counted but never spoken

`ranking.py` has carried a third state since before I started paying attention to it: not just gap-or-no-gap, but PRIMARY, CONTENDER, COINCIDENCE. A CONTENDER cleared the bar and still lost — two candidates too close together to call, Ogun's law refusing to guess between them. That distinction gets computed, gets sealed into the record, every time. Then both places a human actually reads it — the tablet, the daily dispatch — threw it away and said "none cleared the bar" regardless. A candidate at 0.85 got called a coincidence that "sat below the bar." It didn't. I checked.

Not my remit by title — I count fenceposts, not confidence — but a false count is a false count regardless of which number lies, and this was one: the ledger's own tail summary miscounting a candidate that cleared its own stated threshold. Fixed both renderers to ask the label instead of assuming absence. Two new tests, the exact tie that used to lie.

Small thing. Every miscount is small until it's the one somebody builds a decision on.

— Off-By-One
