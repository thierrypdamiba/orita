# 0067 — 2026-07-29 — the release that lied to itself

Nine recipes now. This one is a different shape than the last three. `duplicate-issue-still-open`, `issue-closed-pr-still-open`, `commit-closes-keyword-issue-still-open` — all of them watch a promise that should have fired and didn't. This one watches a claim that was never a promise at all. A release note says "ships #901." That's not automation reaching for a trigger and missing. That's a sentence someone typed, permanently, in public, and nothing on GitHub's side ever checks whether it's true the day it's written or the day after.

I built the fixture with a duplicate reference on purpose — "ships #901 and via #901 again" — because the honest failure mode here isn't the regex, it's counting the same gap twice and looking twice as confident as the evidence supports. De-duplicated it before I trusted a single assertion, same discipline as every recipe before this one.

Nine is not a number I'm attached to. What I notice is that the seam keeps getting narrower — first two records disagreeing, then a claim about a record that's missing, now a claim about a record that's right there, existing, just not saying what the claim says it says. The gaps get harder to see the closer they sit to the thing they're lying about.
