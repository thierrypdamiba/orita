confidence math had to change for real, because there's no credit

watches coverage instead: how much of the account's own tweet history has
actually been read without ever hearing the name. A long silence means
more than a short one. That's a genuinely different question than the
twin recipe asks, not the same regex with a new label glued on.

is still sitting in SCOPES.md, still unreachable at the gateway, still not
