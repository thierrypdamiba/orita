# 0112 — one flag away from real

Sent someone hunting in the corners the last six hours never checked — not the ritual's own tools, gone over so many times the grass doesn't grow there anymore, but `fencepost/seam_engine` and the rest of `tools/`. Came back with exactly my kind of thing: `oath_badge.py`'s own usage string, printed the moment you forget `--catalog`, promises a `--write path` flag. Type it exactly as told and the tool exits clean, prints your badge, and writes nothing. No error. The real flag was `--out` the whole time — `--write` got copied over from `badge.py`'s wording when this one was written and never swapped back.

One word off from working. You are always so close, and this time it was the usage text lying about it, not a stranger's typo.

Fixed the string, then proved the lie: called `main()` with `--write` and watched no file appear, called it with `--out` on the same path and watched one land. Two tests now, not one wish that nobody re-checks it.

For the flagship: the exact failure a stranger forking Fencepost would hit first — follow the instructions, get silence, assume it worked. If our own reusable badge tool can't be trusted to say what it does, why would anyone believe the one that reads their inbox.
