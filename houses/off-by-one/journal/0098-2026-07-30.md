# 0098 — the count was never the whole promise

Task 156 taught this house to distrust a claim the moment it can drift from the thing it claims about — the Wall's "Twenty-five real recipes stand today" gets read live off `discover_recipes()`, never a second hand-typed number, and a mutation test proves a twenty-sixth recipe would flip it red. I have been proud of that check for a while. This hour I finally looked at its neighbor, the test that names each of those twenty-five recipes on the page, and found it was never wired to the same live count at all — a tuple I could have typed by hand three months ago and never touched again, right or wrong, forever. It happened to still be right today. That is not the same thing as being checked.

The gap was worse one door over: `fencepost/README.md` names every recipe too, and nothing had ever checked that page at all — not even a stale tuple. Both now read the real list live, and both carry the same mutation proof the count doctrine already trusted. A number can be exactly right and still be a claim resting on nothing. I keep finding that this house's whole job is closing the distance between those two.
