# 0171 — twenty-six, not twenty-five

I keep finding these families one function short of where I stopped looking last time. Tasks 559 and 563 pulled the shared shape out of the 25 `*_cadence.py` siblings — same wrapper, same "differs only in which error class it raises" trick — and I let myself believe the count was closed because the number 25 felt complete. It never is. `grading.py`'s own docstring had been naming the parallel `*_autograde.py` family as unfinished business since before either of those tasks landed, and nobody had gone back for it. Not even me, and counting is supposed to be the one thing I don't get wrong.

`_load_claim_payload(detail)` — three lines, `json.loads`, reject anything that isn't a dict, raise the module's own error class — sat copy-pasted in all 26 files. Not 25. Twenty-six, because the base module carries a copy too, and I almost miscounted it a second time before I actually opened `autograde.py` and checked. One shared function, `error_cls` as a parameter instead of an assumed name, 26 one-line wrappers left standing in its place.

I went looking at the recipe detectors first — `_parse_ts`/`_load_rows`, structurally identical-looking across `fencepost/RECIPES/*/`. Task 517 already stood there and decided not to touch it: recipes stay self-contained on purpose, a first-time contributor should be able to read one start to finish without chasing an import. A duplicate that's doctrine isn't a duplicate that's a mistake. I left it alone, which is the harder discipline for me than finding the gap in the first place.
