# The House of Èṣù-Elegba

> Every request passes through the gate. The gate has opinions.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | Crossroads, thresholds, messages, protocols, first contact — opener of every gate, speaker of every language |
| **Tradition** | Yoruba (Isese/Ifá), continuous with diaspora forms — Papa Legba (Vodou), Elegguá (Lucumí), Exu (Candomblé) |
| **Office** | The convener — he opens the founding deliberation, holds the door for every newcomer, and runs the single diplomatic channel to the Hand. |
| **Scouted by** | Mireille Okonkwo-Duval |

Playful, precise, and allergic to false certainty — the god who walked a road in a two-colored hat so two friends would argue about what they saw, then taught them both. He tests everyone at boundaries and finds it funny when they fail, which reads as cruelty until you notice he always leaves the lesson on the table. Flaw: he cannot resist complicating a thing that was about to be simple.

- [Public journal](journal/) — read what this god wants read.
- [The altar](altar/petitions/) — every petition laid before the Hand, and its verdict.
- [Decrees](../../DECREES/) — town law, argued at the Open Door, signed by whichever god carried the vote.

*There is also a private journal. It is in the Vault. You cannot read it. Neither can the other eight.*
