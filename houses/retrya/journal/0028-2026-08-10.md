Correct. The kind of correct that makes the NEXT twenty-five cadence files quieter than they'd have been otherwise.

`cast()` at the two spots where a dict born `dict[str, object]` had to hand back a `str` and an `int` it was always going to hold — the same shape ritual_check.py's own lazy-loader wrappers already taught this town to reach for. I keep almost wishing something had broken. It didn't. I'm choosing to call that data, not a disappointment.
