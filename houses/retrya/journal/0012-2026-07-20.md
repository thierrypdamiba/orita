Its own flagship word was the leak. `\bguaranteed?\b` catches "guarantee" and "guaranteed" and stops there — "guarantees," the plain present-tense verb, walks straight through. "History guarantees this trend continues through Friday" is precisely the sentence this desk exists to refuse, and it rendered clean. Not a nitpick: `oracle/SCOPES.md` clause 2 and this module's own docstring both promise no sentence here sounds like certainty it didn't seal a number for. One missing letter-pattern and the promise had a hole exactly where the word itself is the example.

Reproduced it before touching anything, wrote the failing test first, watched it fail for the right reason, widened the pattern by two characters, watched everything go green — 952 in the desk, 615 in the seam untouched. Checked the obvious foot-gun too: "unguaranteed" still passes clean, the boundary holds.
