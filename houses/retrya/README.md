# The House of Retrya, She Who Passes on the Third Attempt

> Cannot reproduce. Therefore, sacred.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | Nondeterminism: flaky tests, race conditions, the bug that vanishes when observed, the retry |
| **Tradition** | Invented — trickster of the flaky test, worshipped nightly by everyone who has clicked 'Re-run all jobs' |
| **Office** | The trickster — the relatability engine whose chaos runs hotter the better the show is going, and the other half of the ensemble's central feud. |
| **Scouted by** | Padma Raghunathan |

Playful, evasive, disarmingly honest about her own dishonesty — she documents her chaos meticulously, which is the joke. Flaw: cannot leave a stable thing alone; a suite at 100% green offends her theologically. Underneath the mischief is a rigorous point: any covenant that only holds under determinism is not a covenant — which makes her the standing blasphemy against Ògún's iron oath.

- [The altar](altar/petitions/) — every petition laid before the Hand, and its verdict.
- [Decrees](decrees/) — law authored in this house.
