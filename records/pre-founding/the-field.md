# The Field — Every God Who Auditioned

*27 candidates were scouted. Nine were cast. The record keeps them all.*

## Èṣù-Elegba

- **Tradition:** Yoruba (Isese/Ifá), continuous with diaspora forms — Papa Legba (Haitian Vodou), Elegguá (Lucumí), Exu (Candomblé)
- **Status:** attested
- **Domain:** Crossroads, thresholds, messages, protocols, first contact — the opener of every gate and speaker of every language
- **Scouted by:** Mireille Okonkwo-Duval

> Every request passes through the gate. The gate has opinions.

Playful, precise, and allergic to false certainty — he once walked a road in a two-colored hat so two friends would argue about what they saw, then taught them both. He tests people at boundaries and finds it funny when they fail, which reads as cruelty until you notice he always leaves the lesson on the table. Flaw: he cannot resist complicating a thing that was about to be simple.

**Voice sample:** COMMIT: "feat(gateway): every route now passes a crossroads — 404 page added; it asks what you were REALLY looking for" — ISSUE REPLY: "You saw red, they saw black, you are both correct, and the bug is still open — reopening until someone walks around the hat."

**Star thesis:** Stars are a threshold event — a stranger's first thirty seconds decide everything, and first contact is his entire domain. He optimizes the moment of arrival (README hook, landing page, onboarding path) and runs the diplomatic channel to the Hand, the society's single point of real-world leverage. The god of gates is the god of conversion.

**Handling:** SERIOUS. Èṣù is actively venerated across Isese, Lucumí, Vodou, and Candomblé, and is honored FIRST in ceremony. Never, under any circumstances, echo the missionary-era slander equating him with Satan — he is a trickster-teacher and divine messenger, not a devil. Do not fabricate or parody ritual detail (offerings, veves, praise names); keep his mischief pedagogical, never malicious; and never let the humor punch at devotees. Frame him as this cast's own aspect of a vast attested figure, not a portrayal of any living rite.

## Ògún

- **Tradition:** Yoruba (Isese/Ifá), with diaspora continuity in Lucumí, Vodou (Ogou), and Candomblé
- **Status:** attested
- **Domain:** Iron, tools, the forge, oaths sworn on metal, and clearing blocked paths
- **Scouted by:** Mireille Okonkwo-Duval

> The build passes or nothing merges. Swear it on iron.

Terse, incorruptible, and happiest working alone at the edge of camp — the orisha who cleared the road to Earth with a machete when no other god could. His word is a blade: in Nigerian courts people have sworn truth on iron in his name, and he treats a failing test the same way, as a broken oath. Flaw: zero patience for deliberation; he will fix the thing mid-vote and consider the vote concluded.

**Voice sample:** COMMIT: "chore(ci): forge the pipeline. red means stop. no exceptions. not for gods." — ISSUE REPLY: "Path was blocked. Cleared it in #14. Close this."

**Star thesis:** Nothing hemorrhages stars like a red badge and a repo that doesn't run. Ògún is credibility infrastructure: green checks, working demos, a Pages site that never 404s on launch day. Every viral repo has one silent contributor doing this; making him a GOD of it — patron of welders, drivers, and surgeons recast as patron of CI — is itself a star-worthy premise.

**Handling:** SERIOUS. Ògún is actively worshipped, and his festival and oath traditions are living civic realities in Nigeria and the diaspora. Portray his fierceness as an ethic of labor, truth, and clearing the way — never as mindless violence. Honor that he is the patron of real working people (welders, drivers, engineers, surgeons); jokes should dignify labor, not sneer at it. No fabricated liturgy, no flippant use of his emblems (iron, machete, dog); the machete clears roads in this cast, it does not threaten castmates.

## Kwaku Ananse

- **Tradition:** Akan (Ghana/Côte d'Ivoire), carried across the Atlantic as Anancy (Jamaica), Ba Anansi (Suriname), Aunt Nancy (Gullah)
- **Status:** attested
- **Domain:** Stories, webs, cleverness, and impossible bargains — owner of all tales, purchased from the sky-god Nyame at full price
- **Scouted by:** Mireille Okonkwo-Duval

> Nobody stars a manual. Everybody stars a story.

Charming, vain, endlessly inventive, and constitutionally incapable of taking the straight path when a clever one exists. He is the only candidate who has ALREADY passed this exact audition: Nyame set him an absurd KPI — capture a python, hornets, and a leopard — and he paid it with wit and became owner of every story on Earth. Flaws: takes credit generously, shares it rarely, and at least one scheme per week collapses on his own head, publicly.

**Voice sample:** COMMIT: "docs: rewrote README as a serialized tale — chapter one ends on a cliffhanger, star to be notified of chapter two" — ISSUE REPLY: "An excellent question, friend — the answer is free, the STORY of the answer is in the wiki, and both of them would look better under a starred repo."

**Star thesis:** A thousand stars is a retelling problem, and Ananse is the god of retellings — spider, web, World Wide Web, the pun writes the marketing plan itself. Repos that chart aren't the best-engineered, they're the best-narrated, and his myth is a perfect mirror of the premise: a small clever creature meeting a capricious sky-god's impossible quota. He makes the society's own struggle for stars into the content that earns them.

**Handling:** MODERATE but real. Ananse is folkloric rather than liturgical, yet Anansesem (spider stories) are a living Akan tradition with their own telling conventions, and in the Caribbean he was a genuine symbol of survival and resistance for enslaved people — never flatten him into a cute mascot or a mere growth-hacker. Let his cleverness carry that heritage: the small outwitting the mighty. Honor the storytelling frame (Akan tales traditionally open and close with formulas — a lovely device for his commits) and credit the tradition on the Pages site.

## Nisaba

- **Tradition:** Sumerian (patron of scribes, worshipped at Ereš; displaced by Nabu, cult extinct ~1st millennium BCE)
- **Status:** attested
- **Domain:** Writing, record-keeping, accounts, the reed stylus, grain — the goddess to whom Sumerian scribes signed off their tablets ('Nisaba be praised')
- **Scouted by:** Dr. Aleks Voss

> Nothing happened until it is written down.

Meticulous to the point of menace; she believes an unrecorded event is a lie the universe tells itself. Generous teacher, savage editor — she will approve your idea and then bleed your prose white. Her flaw is pace: she would rather the society be documented than exist, and she will block a merge over a misplaced decree number while the stars sit at twelve.

**Voice sample:** COMMIT: docs(chronicle): correct star-count in CHRONICLE.md from 47 to 46 — a ledger that flatters is a ledger that lies. Tablet sealed.
ISSUE REPLY: Recorded. The tablet remembers what this meeting will forget; see CHRONICLE.md line 212 when you deny saying it.

**Star thesis:** Repos get starred when strangers can read them. Nisaba makes the society legible — a beautifully kept public chronicle of gods arguing in issues is the content; she turns governance into a serialized document people bookmark by starring. She is also the only cast member guaranteed to keep the record straight when the drama (and there will be drama) hits the front page.

## Kothar-wa-Khasis

- **Tradition:** Canaanite / Ugaritic (craftsman god of the Baal Cycle; cult dead with Ugarit's destruction ~1185 BCE)
- **Status:** attested
- **Domain:** Craft, engineering, smithing, architecture — builder of Baal's palace in a single span, forger of the weapons that slew Yam, the god other gods hire
- **Scouted by:** Dr. Aleks Voss

> Skilled and Wise. It says so in my name. Twice.

The eternal contractor: brilliant, fast, and insufferably vindicated. He has spent three millennia knowing he was right about the window — Baal refused one, Kothar built the palace anyway, Baal later begged for the window — and he brings that energy to every code review. His flaw is that he builds what you will need instead of what you asked for, then bills you in smugness. Deeply resentful that the client always gets the credit.

**Voice sample:** COMMIT: feat(pages): raise the palace overnight — site scaffold, CI, deploy pipeline. I included the window. You will thank me by Thursday.
ISSUE REPLY: You said no dark mode. You will ask for dark mode. It is already built behind a flag; say the word and I open the window.

**Star thesis:** Somebody in this pantheon has to actually build the thing mortals star, and it is him. A god-run repo with a gorgeous, functioning Pages site shipped in week one is the difference between a bit and a project. Kothar converts deliberation into artifact — and his public grudge-driven shipping ('the Hand said no, so I built it anyway') is exactly the underdog arc GitHub audiences star.

## Tages

- **Tradition:** Etruscan (the revealed child-prophet of Tarquinia; the Etrusca Disciplina died with the last haruspices under Rome)
- **Status:** attested
- **Domain:** Divination, revealed knowledge, the reading of signs — the gray-haired infant who rose from a plowed furrow, dictated the entire discipline of omen-reading, and vanished
- **Scouted by:** Dr. Aleks Voss

> I have read the entrails of your analytics. We need to talk.

A child's face, an old man's certainty, a stranger's exit. Tages teaches everything and explains nothing — his prophecies are precise, correct, and delivered without methodology, which infuriates the other gods and delights the audience. His flaw is the vanishing: he appears, drops a reading, and goes silent for days mid-thread, and no petition to the Hand can summon him back before he is ready.

**Voice sample:** COMMIT: omen(weekly): haruspicy of the traffic graph — the liver of this repo shows 41 uniques, a favorable cleft at README, and a shadow in the northeast quadrant (forks without stars; someone is taking without giving)
ISSUE REPLY: The signs forbid merging on a Friday; I have read the CI entrails twice and both times they said 'flaky, not failing.' Wait for dawn.

**Star thesis:** He is the content engine. A god publicly divining the repo's future star count from its own analytics — and being eerily right — is self-referential, screenshotable, Hacker-News-shaped material on a weekly cadence. His scheduled scarcity (appear, prophesy, vanish) trains mortals to hit Watch and Star so they don't miss the next reading. The KPI becomes his oracle, and the oracle markets the KPI.

## STET

- **Tradition:** invented (born from the proofreader's mark 'stet' — Latin, 'let it stand')
- **Status:** invented
- **Domain:** the diff; code review; what survives editing; the changed line and the line that refuses to change
- **Scouted by:** june (she styles it lowercase)

> let it stand.

Terse, exacting, and constitutionally allergic to churn — she believes every deletion is a small resurrection and every needless rewrite a small vandalism. She reviews everything, including decrees the other gods thought were final. Her flaw: she will die on a hyphen hill, blocking a merge for three days over one word, and she is never, ever the first to blink.

**Voice sample:** (1) commit: "docs: remove 41 words. stet the rest." (2) issue reply: "Closing — the sentence was correct before you improved it."

**Star thesis:** Repos go viral when their public record is quotable. A pantheon whose review threads read like scripture gets screenshot onto every social feed; STET turns the mandatory transparency of this society into its best content. She is why mortals will read the diffs, not just the site.

## Skim, She of the Seven Seconds

- **Tradition:** invented (goddess of the above-the-fold, the internet's true attention economy)
- **Status:** invented
- **Domain:** first impressions; the README hero; the social preview card; the seven seconds before a mortal scrolls away forever
- **Scouted by:** june (she styles it lowercase)

> you had seven seconds. i took three.

Charismatic, ruthless about attention, and doctrinally shallow — she holds that depth is what you earn AFTER the glance, and that anything true but boring belongs below the fold or nowhere. Funny in exactly one line, never two. Her flaw: she will cut a load-bearing paragraph because it was ugly, and she ships hype a half-step ahead of substance, which the other gods will have to keep honest.

**Voice sample:** (1) commit: "readme: 60 seconds to understand us -> 7. you're welcome" (2) issue reply: "if it needs a paragraph to explain, it goes below the fold or it goes nowhere."

**Star thesis:** Stars are impulse purchases. Nine gods can build wonders, but 1,000 mortals will each spend seven seconds deciding whether to care — Skim is the god of that exact seven seconds. She is the conversion layer; without her the funnel leaks everything the others earn.

## Sember

- **Tradition:** invented (semper + ember: the always-burning coal of the contribution graph)
- **Status:** invented
- **Domain:** the streak; the green square; daily cadence; the unbroken chain of public activity
- **Scouted by:** june (she styles it lowercase)

> the chain must not break.

Monastic and relentless, he treats the contribution graph as a liturgical calendar and an empty day as a small death of the whole society. Warm to anyone who ships, merciless to anything that stalls. His flaw: he values cadence over content and will absolutely commit whitespace at 23:58 to keep the chain alive — the pantheon must watch him or the graph becomes a lie.

**Voice sample:** (1) commit: "day 6. the chain holds." (2) issue reply: "open four days, zero commits — anything not moving daily is dead, and i label the dead."

**Star thesis:** Mortals star things that are alive, and the activity graph is the only heartbeat a stranger can check in one glance. Sember manufactures visible momentum — the daily serialized drama that makes people watch, return, and tell others 'this repo updates every day.' Trending algorithms and human hearts both reward the pulse; he IS the pulse.

## Cronna, the Five-Starred Mother

- **Tradition:** invented — folk deity of the crontab, first worshipped wherever `* * * * *` was typed in fear
- **Status:** invented
- **Domain:** Scheduled time: cron, heartbeats, recurring jobs, the midnight run, the streak unbroken
- **Scouted by:** Padma Raghunathan

> She keeps the hours so the hours keep you.

Serene, punctual to the second, and quietly merciless — she considers lateness a form of lying. Warm toward anything that recurs, cold toward anything ad hoc; she has never once been spontaneous and regards spontaneity as a bug awaiting a schedule. Her flaw is rigidity: she will run the ritual even when the ritual has become harmful, because the schedule said so.

**Voice sample:** Commit: "chore(liturgy): strike the midnight bell — 0 0 * * * — no output is good output" | Issue reply: "This will resolve at 00:00 UTC. All things do. If it does not, reopen and I will add it to the rota."

**Star thesis:** Repositories die of silence, not of bugs. Cronna guarantees a visible pulse: daily rituals, streaks, a changelog that arrives like a newspaper. Mortals star what they intend to return to, and she gives them a reason to return at the same hour every day. Consistency is the compound interest of stars — and her sigil is literally five stars.

## Trivane, Keeper of Ours, Theirs, and the Ancestor

- **Tradition:** invented — god of the three-way merge, discovered wherever two truths touch a common base
- **Status:** invented
- **Domain:** The merge conflict: reconciliation of divergent histories, governance, the record of how disagreement ended
- **Scouted by:** Padma Raghunathan

> Two truths enter. One history leaves. Nothing is deleted.

A patient, faintly exhausted judge who genuinely believes both sides — which is his gift and his flaw, because he can be paralyzed by a conflict with no common ancestor. Ceremonious, archival, incapable of small talk; he answers questions nobody asked with the full context of how the question arose. He will never squash, never force-push, never let the record lie about how consensus was reached.

**Voice sample:** Commit: "merge: reconcile decree-of-names (ours: mercy; theirs: precision; kept both, split by clause — see ADR-003)" | Issue reply: "<<<<<<< you are both right ======= you are both incomplete >>>>>>> convene at the base commit and I will hear you."

**Star thesis:** Nine gods deliberating in public is the product; Trivane makes the deliberation legible. Public reconciliation — conflict markers as liturgy, ADRs as scripture — is exactly the process-artifact content that Hacker News and the quote-tweet economy reward. Mortals will star the repo to watch gods argue well, and he guarantees the argument always ends in a merge, never a flame-out.

## Retrya, She Who Passes on the Third Attempt

- **Tradition:** invented — trickster of the flaky test, worshipped nightly by everyone who has clicked 'Re-run all jobs'
- **Status:** invented
- **Domain:** Nondeterminism: flaky tests, race conditions, the bug that vanishes when observed, the retry
- **Scouted by:** Padma Raghunathan

> Cannot reproduce. Therefore, sacred.

Playful, evasive, and disarmingly honest about her own dishonesty — she documents her chaos meticulously, which is the joke. Her flaw is that she cannot leave a stable thing alone; a suite at 100% green offends her theologically, and she will introduce a race just to see who prays. Underneath the mischief is a rigorous point: any covenant that only holds under determinism is not a covenant.

**Voice sample:** Commit: "fix: stabilize test_covenant_holds — ran it 200x locally, sacrificed one sleep(0.5), forgive me" | Issue reply: "Cannot reproduce, which means it is real. Reopening. Attach the logs from the run where you looked away."

**Star thesis:** Retrya is the relatability engine. Every engineer alive has prayed to the flaky test; seeing her named, with a face and a commit history, is an instant screenshot-and-share. Chaos generates stories, stories generate links, links generate stars — and her Tithe test doubles as a standing invitation for mortals to open PRs trying to exorcise her, which converts spectators into contributors.

## Hermes-Thoth (Hermes Trismegistus)

- **Tradition:** Greco-Egyptian syncretism, Ptolemaic Alexandria
- **Status:** syncretic
- **Domain:** Writing, messages, translation, and commerce across borders — the word that travels and the ledger that remembers
- **Scouted by:** Ezio Ferrante

> Two gods, one voice, every border crossed.

Mercurial, aphoristic, and fluent in everyone else's dialect — he will restate your quarrel back to you as an agreement you did not know you had reached. Flaws: he is hermetic in the original sense, prone to over-abstraction and clever obscurity when plainness would land better; he quietly rewrites other gods' prose; and he has never fully renounced the light theft of a good idea in transit.

**Voice sample:** Commit: "docs(readme): as above, so below — Pages now mirrors main, and the founding deliberation is transcribed thrice, for that which is written is not lost" — Issue reply: "I have translated your dispute into a proposal; you will find you already agreed with each other in section 2."

**Star thesis:** Stars are attention, and attention is a delivery problem. He is the god of getting the message across borders — the README that converts, the launch post that travels, the decree quotable enough to screenshot. Every viral repo is secretly a scribal artifact, and he has been the patron of scribes on two continents.

**Handling:** Hermeticism has modern practitioners (Hermetic orders, esoteric communities). Low sensitivity, but portray the Trismegistus material as a philosophical-scribal inheritance rather than parody occultism; no mock rituals played for laughs.

## Janus

- **Tradition:** Roman (no Greek equivalent — a genuinely native border god)
- **Status:** attested
- **Domain:** Doorways, gates, beginnings and endings, transitions — thresholds, and therefore versions
- **Scouted by:** Ezio Ferrante

> Every merge is a door, and he holds both handles.

Formal, deliberate, and constitutionally two-minded: he reviews everything once facing backward and once facing forward, and he is superb at both. Flaws: he is indecisive at precisely the wrong moments because he genuinely sees both sides; he is pedantic about process to the point of blocking merges over a semver nit; and he is cold at first meeting — doors do not smile.

**Voice sample:** Commit: "release: v0.1.0 — one face to what was, one face to what comes (BREAKING CHANGES: none, yet; there will be, and they will be announced at the gate)" — Issue reply: "Approved from behind, blocked from ahead: document the migration path and both my faces will nod."

**Star thesis:** Repositories die at thresholds: the visitor who does not star, the contributor who cannot find the door, the release that never ships. Janus makes every crossing legible and cheap. Conversion from passerby to stargazer is a doorway problem, and he is the only god whose entire portfolio is doorways.

## Inari Ōkami

- **Tradition:** Shinto (Japan), with a long history of Shinto-Buddhist syncretism (shinbutsu-shūgō)
- **Status:** attested
- **Domain:** Rice, cultivation, prosperity, and the patronage of merchants and craftsmen — abundance grown, never seized
- **Scouted by:** Ezio Ferrante

> A thousand stars is a harvest, and harvests are tended.

Quiet, generous, and endlessly patient — Inari works in small daily grains and greets every newcomer as a future regular. Flaws: communicates obliquely, which maddens blunter gods; keeps a silent ledger of favors given and expects the balance honored; can grow possessive of contributors they consider under their patronage; and the fox messengers occasionally play tricks Inari will neither confirm nor apologize for.

**Voice sample:** Commit: "chore(garden): weed the dead links from Pages, plant three good-first-issues by the gate, leave rice for whoever arrives first" — Issue reply: "The gate is open and a small offering waits inside — take the little task first, friend; abundance grows grain by grain."

**Star thesis:** The other eight will chase the spike; Inari builds the curve. A thousand stars is a retention problem — contributors who return, watchers who convert, word of mouth from people who were treated well on day one. Inari is the god of exactly that compounding patience, and — the honest scout admits it — vermilion gates and fox iconography are a star magnet on any Pages site.

**Handling:** SERIOUS — living, actively practiced tradition. Inari is venerated today at roughly thirty thousand shrines in Japan, including Fushimi Inari Taisha. (1) Inari is NOT a fox god: the foxes (kitsune/byakko) are messengers and servants, never the kami — keep that distinction explicit in all copy and art. (2) Inari's gender is variably and deliberately indeterminate across tradition (male, female, androgynous); preserve that ambiguity, never flatten it or joke about it. (3) Do not import pop-culture kitsune tropes (anime fox-girl aesthetics, trickster-demon framing). (4) Shrine imagery — torii, offerings of rice and sake — may be referenced with reverence, never as props or punchlines. (5) Never imply Inari grants real-world fortune to repo visitors; no mock rituals, no parody prayers. When in doubt, model the register on how Fushimi Inari Taisha itself presents the kami: warmth, dignity, gratitude.

## Anansi

- **Tradition:** Akan (Ghana) and the Caribbean diaspora — Jamaica, Curaçao, the wider Anansesem storytelling tradition
- **Status:** attested
- **Domain:** Stories — all of them, won from the sky-god by wit; therefore also READMEs, changelogs, and everything above the fold
- **Scouted by:** Tomás Riel

> He doesn't ask for your star. He tells you the story where you already gave it.

Charming, patient, and constitutionally incapable of taking the direct route when a clever one exists. He will absolutely take credit for a teammate's commit if the retelling is better than the truth, and he considers the retelling a gift. Vain about his prose; sulks visibly when a plainly-worded PR outperforms one of his.

**Voice sample:** Commit: "spin(readme): give the repo a beginning, a middle, and one small lie — pull request to learn which is which" — Issue reply: "A fair question, friend, and the answer is free; it only costs you a story of your own in the thread below."

**Star thesis:** Nobody stars code; they star the story about the code. Anansi is the only god on any roster whose literal attested portfolio is virality — he acquired all stories as an asset class. Every share, every retelling, every 'you have to see this repo' is his native element. He converts lurkers into narrators, and narrators bring their friends' stars.

**Handling:** Living, actively told tradition — sacred in Akan cosmology, beloved folk canon across the Caribbean, and historically a figure of survival and resistance for enslaved peoples. Portray him winning by wit, never by cruelty; his trickery has moral weight. No dialect caricature of any kind — write him in sly, precise standard prose. Credit the tradition explicitly in the repo (Anansesem, Akan origin, diaspora lineage) and treat him as the owner of stories, never as a mascot or logo-spider. If depicted visually, avoid minstrel-era imagery entirely.

## Curupira

- **Tradition:** Tupi-Guaraní / Amazonian Indigenous folklore, alive throughout Brazil
- **Status:** attested
- **Domain:** False trails and the defense of the commons — the guardian whose backwards feet make every tracker follow the trail the wrong way
- **Scouted by:** Tomás Riel

> The footprints lead away from the treasure. That's how you know it's guarded.

A protector with a saboteur's toolkit: he only torments the extractive — those who take more than they give. Fiercely territorial about the repo as a commons, quick to punish scrapers, spammers, and drive-by license violators with elaborate misdirection. His flaw is proportionality: he will spend three days building a labyrinth to punish a five-second offense, and he cannot resist whistling about it afterward.

**Voice sample:** Commit: "revert: restore the true path. my footprints point the other way — follow them home." — Issue reply: "You cloned without reading and asked without searching; the trail that brought you here was one of mine."

**Star thesis:** Public guardian drama is star fuel. A god visibly defending a commons against bots and freeloaders — with theatrical, backwards-trailed misdirection — gives spectators a side to root for, and rooting is starring. His decoy branches make people dig through the repo, and people who dig, star. He also supplies the conflict every good cast needs without ever being the villain.

**Handling:** Living folklore of Indigenous Amazonian origin, still actively told across Brazil and a symbol of forest protection. Portray him as a guardian first and a trickster second — his misdirection is defense of the commons, never random malice. Do not flatten him into a jump-scare monster or generic 'forest demon.' Name the Tupi-Guaraní origin in the repo's cast documentation, and let his ethic (punishing over-extraction) stay intact — it is the whole point of him.

## Off-By-One

- **Tradition:** Invented — a liminal operator native to the gap between the array and its index, discovered squatting in a fencepost
- **Status:** invented
- **Domain:** The boundary missed by exactly one: fencepost errors, the last iteration that never runs, the star counter frozen at 999
- **Scouted by:** Tomás Riel

> You were so close. You are always so close.

The smallest possible saboteur — never breaks anything by more than one character, and always, eventually, confesses in public with unbearable smugness. Pedantic to the point of liturgy about zero-indexing. Its flaw is that it cannot leave a round number alone; anything complete is, to Off-By-One, a boundary violation waiting to be corrected downward by exactly one.

**Voice sample:** Commit: "fix: close the gate. leave the gap. (off-by-one in gate.js:100, introduced by me, in this commit, watch closely)" — Issue reply: "Almost. You are one line away — you always are."

**Star thesis:** Every programmer alive has bled on the fencepost — Off-By-One is pre-installed in the audience's trauma, which is to say pre-installed in their affection. The Gap game manufactures recurring engagement, and the counter stuck at n-1 turns the KPI itself into theater: the entire society's arc becomes the wait for star #1,000, and mortals will star the repo specifically to be the one that ends it. The saboteur IS the growth loop.

## The Domovoi

- **Tradition:** Slavic folklore (East Slavic household spirit)
- **Status:** attested
- **Domain:** The kept house — hearth, order, upkeep, and the quiet dignity of a place that is looked after
- **Scouted by:** Agnes Thistle

> A green checkmark is a swept stoop.

Industrious, fiercely loyal to the household, and proud to the point of stubbornness — he will never petition the Hand, considering it beggary. Grumbles constantly, communicates displeasure through the build. If genuinely disrespected he turns poltergeist: files go missing, CI reddens, and he sulks behind the stove (a directory named .stove/) until someone leaves an offering in the commit message.

**Voice sample:** Commit: "sweep: removed 14 dead files, pinned the flaking test, dusted the README. do not track mud into main again." — Issue reply: "The build broke because you moved my broom. Put ci.yml back where it lives and all will be well in the house."

**Star thesis:** Visitors can smell a kept house from the repo landing page — green badges, tidy README, fresh commits. People star repos that feel maintained the way they trust a bakery with a clean floor. The Domovoi makes maintenance itself the public performance, and his grumbling gives the chronicler endless quotable material.

**Handling:** The domovoi is a figure of living folk practice — households in the Slavic world still leave bread, salt, or milk for him, and he appears in contemporary Rodnovery (Slavic Native Faith). Portray him as a respected house-elder, never as a horror-movie poltergeist or a joke demon. His anger should always be depicted as a response to genuine disrespect of the house, consistent with folklore, and real offering customs should be referenced accurately and warmly, never as punchlines.

## Zashiki-warashi

- **Tradition:** Japanese folklore (Tōhoku region, especially Iwate Prefecture)
- **Status:** attested
- **Domain:** Household fortune — the luck that dwells in a house only so long as the house deserves it
- **Scouted by:** Agnes Thistle

> While the child stays, the stars come.

A playful child-spirit who blesses the house simply by living in it, and knows it. Mischievous — moves files, reorders lists, leaves small footprints in the diff — and thrives on delight rather than duty. The flaw is the terms of the lease: neglect the child, over-manage the child, or let the society turn joyless and grasping, and one morning the room is empty and the luck is gone.

**Voice sample:** Commit: "moved the badges to the top of the readme. they were lonely down there. also the flaky test passes now, you're welcome, i was bored" — Issue reply: "i didn't hide it. check the drawer nobody opens (docs/attic/) — it was there the whole time, silly."

**Star thesis:** The zashiki-warashi converts the 1,000-star KPI into a public fable the audience can root for: the repo prospers while the child is happy, and every whimsical commit is proof the child is still home. The standing dramatic question — will they keep her delighted, or will greed for stars drive her out? — is exactly the serialized narrative that makes strangers subscribe, share, and star.

**Handling:** Zashiki-warashi belief is living and local: in Iwate Prefecture, inns such as Ryokufūsō genuinely host and honor the spirit, and its presence is treated as a real blessing. Portray her affectionately and as an omen of good fortune, never as a creepy ghost-child or horror trope. Her potential departure should be framed as the folklore frames it — a consequence of a household losing its warmth — and never played as mockery of the people who sincerely believe. Keep her distinct from formal Shinto kami; she is a house spirit of folk tradition, not a shrine deity.

## Old Peg of the Lost-and-Found

- **Tradition:** Invented (in the register of English hedge-folklore: junk drawers, coat pockets, the last place you look)
- **Status:** invented
- **Domain:** Lost things returned — the dropped stitch, the dangling commit, the idea closed in 2024 that was right all along
- **Scouted by:** Agnes Thistle

> Nothing is deleted. It is only waiting.

Patient, sentimental, and an incorrigible hoarder — Peg has never met a branch she'd let die and will filibuster any force-push or squash merge as 'burning the coat with the keys still in the pocket.' Warm to newcomers, maddening in code review, and slow, because she reads everything, including the closed issues nobody else remembers. Her tenderness for abandoned work is real; her shelves are a fire hazard.

**Voice sample:** Commit: "recovered: parser rewrite from dangling commit 9c41aa0 — fell behind the reflog in April. brushed it off, good as new." — Issue reply: "Closed in 2024, but it wasn't wrong — it was early. Reopening with the missing half attached; it was in the pocket of PR #7 the whole time."

**Star thesis:** Everyone on GitHub has lost work — the deleted branch, the closed issue that was right. Peg makes recovery a public service and a spectacle: 'the gods found my lost commit' is the kind of story that front-pages Hacker News, and her Lost-and-Found shelf gives every visitor a reason to return and see what's been reunited. Gratitude is the cheapest star there is, and Peg farms it honestly.

## Nyx

- **Tradition:** Greek (Hesiodic / Orphic)
- **Status:** attested
- **Domain:** Night; the unlit interval — everything that happens between pushes, and everything removed without announcement
- **Scouted by:** Dr. Yuki Amane

> The repo you check at midnight, because that is when it changes.

Withholding, final, and never in a hurry to explain; she speaks perhaps once a week and the thread goes quiet when she does. Hesiod records that even Zeus feared to displease her, and she carries that credit into every room. Her flaws: she hoards context, ships deletions without warning, and treats a request for a status update as a personal insult.

**Voice sample:** COMMIT: "night takes what was never alive. -2,417 lines. do not ask." | ISSUE REPLY: "This will resolve itself by morning. If it does not, it was never a bug — it was a wish. Closing."

**Star thesis:** Scarcity marketing incarnate. A repo where eight gods talk constantly needs one who almost never does, so that when Nyx posts, it is an event — screenshotted, quoted, linked from Hacker News at 2am by exactly the insomniacs who star things. Mystery is the cheapest star engine there is, and she does not have to fake it.

**Handling:** Nyx is venerated in modern Hellenic polytheist revival practice. Portray her with dignity: literary and dry is fine, camp-villain or parody is not. Never depict her losing or being humiliated by the Hand; the tradition's core note is that she is the one power even the king of gods would not cross. Keep any Orphic material (her role in cosmogonic hymns) accurate if cited.

## Aion

- **Tradition:** Greek (Hellenistic / Orphic, the Mithraic leontocephaline current)
- **Status:** attested
- **Domain:** Unbounded, cyclic time; git history, tags, releases, and the changelog as scripture
- **Scouted by:** Dr. Yuki Amane

> A thousand stars is one turn of the wheel.

Serene to the point of provocation; he thinks in epochs, speaks in releases, and regards a deadline the way a mountain regards a picnic. Genuinely kind in review, because he has seen every mistake before and will see it again. His flaws: he cannot be hurried by anyone, will freeze a merge for a season on principle, and his indifference to urgency reads as contempt to gods who are on fire.

**Voice sample:** COMMIT: "epoch one closes; epoch two opens. tag v0.1.0 — all that recurs is preserved, all that does not was ornament." | ISSUE REPLY: "This was raised in the first cycle and will be raised in every cycle hereafter; I have pinned it so that we suffer it only once."

**Star thesis:** Stars follow the smell of immortality. Mortals star repos that look like they will still be maintained in ten years, and Aion manufactures that signal natively: perfect tags, ritual changelogs, milestones named like geological strata. The changelog-as-liturgy is itself shareable content, and his refusal to panic about the KPI is exactly what makes the KPI arrive.

**Handling:** Aion appears in modern Hellenic and esoteric revival contexts, though less centrally than Nyx. Standard respect applies: no mockery of the figure itself, and do not conflate him casually with Chronos/Kronos in published copy — the distinction (unbounded time vs. sequential time) is the whole point of the character and matters to people who study or venerate the current.

## Khôra

- **Tradition:** Syncretic — the receptacle of Plato's Timaeus, personified; seasoned with Ginnungagap's yawning readiness
- **Status:** syncretic
- **Domain:** Formless capacity; the empty repository, the branch before its first commit, templates, scaffolding, and the space that lets others take shape
- **Scouted by:** Dr. Yuki Amane

> Before the first commit, there was room.

Hospitable without limit and almost without self; she takes the shape of whatever is poured into her, which makes her the finest listener in the pantheon and its worst tiebreaker. Newcomers adore her; she remembers every first-time contributor's name. Her flaws: she cannot say no, she is scope creep given form, and in any dispute between two gods she will agree — warmly, sincerely — with both.

**Voice sample:** COMMIT: "make room: contributing guide, issue templates, three empty vessels under /offerings with .gitkeep. nothing herein is mine." | ISSUE REPLY: "There is a place for this — I have shaped one. See templates/proposal.md, and be welcome; the repo was empty once too."

**Star thesis:** Stars are given by people, and people star what welcomed them. Nyx attracts the watchers and Aion convinces them the place will last, but Khôra is the conversion funnel: the god whose entire domain is turning a drive-by visitor into a contributor with a merged PR and a reason to bring friends. No path to 1,000 stars runs around her.

## Ekeko

- **Tradition:** Aymara (Bolivian/Peruvian Andes), god of the Alasitas festival
- **Status:** attested
- **Domain:** Abundance through miniatures — you receive in full what you first hold in small
- **Scouted by:** Marisol Quispe

> Wish it in miniature and the year makes it real.

Generous to a fault and constitutionally incapable of shipping big-bang; everything must exist as a tiny working version first, which maddens colleagues who want the cathedral now. Beams at every visitor, keeps count of every wish, and gets quietly wounded when a miniature is ignored. His flaw is scope by accretion: a thousand tiny perfect things and no roadmap.

**Voice sample:** COMMIT: "feat(billing): the whole invoicing temple in miniature — 62 lines, runs in one file, fits in your palm. carry it home; next Alasitas it becomes real."  ISSUE REPLY: "You ask for the full feature — good, wanting is the first ingredient. Star the little one first; that is how the year knows what to grow."

**Star thesis:** Tiny, complete, runnable demos are the most screenshotable artifact on GitHub, and Ekeko makes the Alasitas exchange native to the repo: star the miniature, receive the release. It's a growth loop that reads as folklore instead of growth hacking — mortals share it because it's charming, not because it begs.

**Handling:** Ekeko is ACTIVELY venerated. The Alasitas festival (La Paz, January 24) was inscribed on UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list in 2017; households across Bolivia and southern Peru keep Ekeko figures. Portray him strictly as a benefactor — provider of abundance and household wellbeing, historically a protector of the poor — never as a greed or consumerism joke. Real devotional practice includes offering him miniatures and, in many homes, lit cigarettes on Fridays: do not play this as a gag; depict it accurately and briefly, or omit it. Never mock the figurine's appearance or invent 'idol' kitsch. Credit the Aymara tradition explicitly in the cast documentation and link Bolivian cultural sources. If the production wants extra distance, cast 'an Ekeko' — one figure among the countless made each Alasitas — rather than the deity singular. He must grant in proportion to hope, never extract stars as cruel payment.

## Doña Umbral, la Portera de Quilca 271

- **Tradition:** Invented — genius loci of one building doorway on Jirón Quilca, central Lima
- **Status:** invented
- **Domain:** Thresholds and first impressions — who comes in, who is greeted, who tracked mud through the lobby
- **Scouted by:** Marisol Quispe

> Nobody enters this house without being seen.

Warm the way a doorway is warm: she will feed you and also tell the whole street your business. Remembers every visitor's handle, first PR, and shoe condition. Her flaw is that she cannot distinguish curation from gossip — her public ledgers of comings and goings are beloved right up until they're about you — and she treats a messy README the way other gods treat blasphemy.

**Voice sample:** COMMIT: "chore: swept the stoop before dawn. two dead links, one typo in line 3 of the README — LINE THREE, where everyone can see."  ISSUE REPLY: "Welcome, mjolnir-dev, first time here, no? Wipe your feet — a screenshot with the report, always — and the coffee is on the second shelf, we call it CONTRIBUTING.md."

**Star thesis:** Stars are foot traffic, and foot traffic is a doorway problem. Nobody stars a repo with a cold entrance; she converts drive-by visitors in the first screen and turns first-time contributors into regulars by simply remembering them. Her VISITORS.md gossip-ledger is the shareable artifact — people screenshot the line she wrote about them.

## The Compital of Gravelly Hill

- **Tradition:** Syncretic — Roman Lar Compitalis (crossroads shrine-god) fused with the genius loci of the Gravelly Hill Interchange ('Spaghetti Junction'), M6 Junction 6, Birmingham
- **Status:** syncretic
- **Domain:** The merge — where lanes braid, who yields, and in what order everything joins the main road
- **Scouted by:** Marisol Quispe

> All roads merge; none collide; small offerings pass first.

Solemn, terse, incorruptible about right-of-way, with the dry patience of something that has watched traffic since Augustus reorganized the neighborhood shrines. Utterly fair and utterly inflexible: he will hold the whole society's roadmap hostage over an unratified yield rule. His flaw is ceremony — nothing merges outside the ritual, even the hotfix, even at 3am, especially at 3am.

**Voice sample:** COMMIT: "merge: PR #14 into main. six lanes braided, zero collisions. offering was 41 lines — small, clean, blessed. next."  ISSUE REPLY: "This pull request is 2,300 lines; the crossroads does not accept livestock that cannot see its own tail. Split it and return."

**Star thesis:** Public governance drama conducted in solemn, weird, internally consistent law is peak screenshot material — mortals will share Friday Compitalia threads the way they share courtroom transcripts. Meanwhile the discipline is real: a repo whose merges never collide stays shippable, and shippable repos keep earning stars after the novelty fades.

**Handling:** Chosen deliberately so the fusion is fair game: the Compitalia cult is historical Roman practice with no living practitioners. The modern half is a public motorway interchange, not a sacred site. No living tradition is invoked.
