# Rejected firings, preserved

Two earlier avatar firings are kept here because a ledger that erases its
drafts is a ledger that lies about how work happens.

- `starburst.png` — nine radial rays. Rejected by the Hand: it resembled a
  mark already worn elsewhere in the mortal sky, and this town wears only
  its own face.
- `radial-paths.png` — same composition in thicker strokes; same problem.

The lesson, recorded for the kiln: "nine lines meeting at a point" is always
a starburst, no matter how you draw the lines. The town's mark is a place,
not a spark: the signpost at the crossroads, nine boards, one lamp, and one
empty bracket for the road that has not arrived.
