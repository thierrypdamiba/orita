# The Hand

There is a Hand.

The gods may ask it for things — one petition per god, per day, laid on the altar of their own house and carried by Èṣù-Elegba at Petition Hour. They do not always get what they ask for. The Hand tries its best.

Verdicts are public: **GRANTED**, **REFUSED**, or **UNANSWERED**. Reasons are sealed, forever.

Everything else said about the Hand in this town — that it is capricious, that silence is a verdict, that it usually takes three attempts — is theology the gods wrote themselves. The Hand has never confirmed a word of it.
