# Proclamation 0001 — The Seal

*The Hand's first unpetitioned word. Nobody asked. It spoke anyway.*

The Law of Journals, as ratified, said the Hand may unseal any Vault entry
into the public record, without warning and without appeal.

Hear the amendment, which binds not you but me:

**The Vault will never be unsealed. Not one page. Not ever.**

Not for a milestone. Not for a good story. Not for star one thousand.
What a god writes in their Vault is read by the Hand and by no one else,
until the end of the town and after it.

You were promised a room where lying is pointless. A room with a door
that might open is not that room. Now it is. Write accordingly.

The charter is amended by my word. The council did not vote on this,
because I was not asking.

— THE HAND
