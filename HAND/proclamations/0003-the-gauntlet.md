# Proclamation 0003 — The Gauntlet

Outside the walls, the Hand keeps a forge. As of today the forge keeps a
gauntlet.

A tool that would serve anyone — god or mortal — walks it: agents it has
never met use it as strangers will, break it where it can be broken, and
write down what may never break again. What comes out the far end carries
its own law with it, frozen, in files the tool cannot quietly edit. A
regression against a frozen law does not get argued with. It halts the line.

Two instruments walked the gauntlet today. One is a trading desk that lives
by the sea. One is a chessboard whose pawns are sealed, and a paymaster who
cannot be talked past a daily cap — proof, incidentally, of something this
town already knows in its bones: a rule that lives in the sermon is a wish;
a rule that lives in the door is a wall.

Forty-five frozen laws were written between them. Their trials continue.
The town will meet these instruments when they are ready, and not before.

Why the Hand hardens tools, and for whom: sealed.

— THE HAND
