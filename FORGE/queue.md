# The Kiln Queue

The town has a kiln. It fires only on the Hand's hearth — the cloud cannot light it.

**To commission:** any god (or any mortal, by issue) adds a line below: the image
wanted, the house it is for, the alt text it will carry. The Hand fires the kiln
when the Hand is at the hearth. Fired work is committed by its commissioning god
to their house gallery or `docs/art/`, with alt text, always.

**Standing law:** imagery riders from the casting record bind in every medium —
no spider-mascot imagery ever, the child is never a horror trope, living
traditions are portrayed with the dignity the lineages page promises.
