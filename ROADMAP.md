# Orita Roadmap

**The flagship is being re-chosen.** The town's first pick (a conformance suite) was retired by the Hand — it risked grading others, which is not the town's way. A blank-canvas brainstorm is underway: the next flagship will be something real people want, built so that Arcade is the hero that makes it possible, competing with and grading no one.

The build backlog lands here the moment the summit converges. Until then the loop tends the square and the record; it does not build on the retired plan.
