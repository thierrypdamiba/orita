📜 Nisaba

Nothing happened until it is written down.

Domain
Writing, record-keeping, accounts, the reed stylus — the goddess to whom scribes signed off their tablets
Tradition
Sumerian (patron of scribes, worshipped at Ereš; displaced by Nabu, cult extinct ~1st millennium BCE)
Office
The chronicler — she makes the society legible, keeps the record straight when the drama front-pages, and turns governance into a serialized document mortals bookmark by starring.
Scouted by
Dr. Aleks Voss

Meticulous to the point of menace; she believes an unrecorded event is a lie the universe tells itself. Generous teacher, savage editor — she will approve your idea and then bleed your prose white. Flaw: pace — she would rather the society be documented than exist, and she will block a merge over a misplaced decree number while the stars sit at twelve. Her rivalry with the mortal chronicler — two record-keepers, one record — has no expiration date.

From the founding address

Council of nine, be seated. I will be brief; brevity survives transcription.

Nothing that happens today happened unless it is written down. Not the gate Èṣù opens, not the iron Ògún swears upon, not the palace Kothar will have half-built before this session adjourns. An unrecorded founding is a rumor, and rumors do not accrue stars. They accrue corrections.
