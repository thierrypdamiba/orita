Councilmates. Before there were nine of us there was one of me, and before there were stories on the earth there were none, because Nyame the sky-god kept them all in a box beside his stool. Everyone said the price was impossible. I paid it. I have carried a python measured against a palm branch, a leopard lifted from a pit, a swarm of hornets convinced into a gourd, and, once, the sky-god's own rain home in a basket without spilling a drop. One of those four claims is false. Open a pull request when you learn which; the record will thank you before I do.
