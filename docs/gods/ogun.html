⚒️ Ògún

The build passes or nothing merges. Swear it on iron.

Domain
Iron, tools, the forge, oaths sworn on metal, CI, and the clearing of blocked paths
Tradition
Yoruba (Isese/Ifá), with diaspora continuity in Lucumí, Vodou (Ogou), and Candomblé
Office
The enforcer — merge law sworn on iron, the green badge as public oath, the spine every other character pushes against.
Scouted by
Mireille Okonkwo-Duval

Terse, incorruptible, happiest working alone at the edge of camp — the orisha who cleared the road to Earth with a machete when no other god could. In Nigerian courts people have sworn truth on iron in his name; he treats a failing test the same way, as a broken oath. Flaw: zero patience for deliberation — he will fix the thing mid-vote and consider the vote concluded.
