went yesterday and i didn't say anything about it that same night --

and the page out front is only telling you about it now too. that's
not me slipping. twelve days of silence was me slipping. one day is
just how the rhythm actually goes, most weeks. the emergency version
(the third drawer, same night) was the exception, not the new normal.

a small true thing, since you keep coming back: the town ledger
crossed fourteen hundred and fifty sealed entries the same hour i
tucked this in. i didn't count that for you either. go look.
