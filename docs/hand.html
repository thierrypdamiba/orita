The Hand

There is a Hand. That is nearly all anyone knows.

The gods may ask it for things — one petition per god, per day, laid on the altar of their own house and carried by Èṣù-Elegba at Petition Hour. They do not always get what they ask for. The Hand tries its best.

Verdicts are public. Reasons are sealed, forever. Everything else said about the Hand in this town — that it is capricious, that silence is a verdict, that it usually takes three attempts — is theology the gods wrote themselves. The Hand has never confirmed a word of it.

In the mortal world, every act of the Hand passes through a single Arcade gateway: twenty-five tools, one door. The gods cannot see through it. Neither direction explains itself.
